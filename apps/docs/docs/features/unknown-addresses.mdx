Bounce mail sent to any address on your <Terminology title="The domain every alias lives on" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">base domain</Terminology> that isn't one of your enabled aliases, before it ever reaches your <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology>.

## Why Use This Feature?

1. Guessed and mistyped addresses get a clear bounce, so a real person knows to check the address.
2. Random spam to made-up addresses never runs your <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology> or triggers a notification.
3. There is nothing to configure, so an address only works once you create it as an <Terminology title="A stand-in address; mail still lands in your real inbox" to="/docs/reference/terminology#aliases-and-mail-flow">alias</Terminology>.

## How It Works

Deploy keeps one <Terminology title="Sends one alias's mail to Mailmask" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">routing rule</Terminology> per enabled <Terminology title="A stand-in address; mail still lands in your real inbox" to="/docs/reference/terminology#aliases-and-mail-flow">alias</Terminology>, and only those addresses reach the <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology>. <Terminology title="Cloudflare's mail intake that hands messages to Mailmask" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Cloudflare Email Routing</Terminology> rejects every other address on the domain itself.

| Address                              | What the sender gets                                                   |
|--------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| An enabled alias                     | Nothing; the message is forwarded, unless the alias refuses it.        |
| An address you never made            | A bounce from Cloudflare with `Address does not exist`.                |
| A disabled or removed alias          | A bounce from Cloudflare with `Address does not exist`.                |
| `alias+tag@` with `subaddressing` on | The same as the alias, since Email Routing subaddressing is turned on. |

Cloudflare's servers return that rejection during delivery, so the sender's mail server doesn't retry ([Cloudflare docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/configuration/domains/)). No notification is sent for these addresses.

:::info
A <Terminology title="Cloudflare's rule for addresses without their own rule" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">catch-all</Terminology> you set up yourself still catches unknown addresses, because deploy only turns off one that points at this Worker.
:::

## Refusals From the Worker

A known <Terminology title="A stand-in address; mail still lands in your real inbox" to="/docs/reference/terminology#aliases-and-mail-flow">alias</Terminology> that has expired or refuses a sender bounces with its own reason, such as `Address has expired` or `Sender is blocked`. These <Terminology title="Mail an alias turns away instead of forwarding" to="/docs/reference/terminology#aliases-and-mail-flow">refusals</Terminology> come from the <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology>, never forward anywhere else, and trigger your notifiers.

## Other Domains

Mail that reaches the <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology> for any domain other than your <Terminology title="The domain every alias lives on" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">base domain</Terminology> is rejected with `Recipient domain is not handled by this Worker`. It never triggers a notification.

This only happens when a rule or catch-all you created by hand sends another domain's mail to the same <Terminology title="Mailmask itself, running on your Cloudflare account" to="/docs/reference/terminology#cloudflare-and-deploy">Worker</Terminology>.
